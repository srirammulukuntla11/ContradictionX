[SECTION 1: STATUTORY AUDIT & DATA RETENTION]
REQ-101: The system must log every state-changing API request, monetary transfer, and authorization grant in an append-only audit ledger.
REQ-102: All ledger entries must store the initiating user identity, IP address, timestamp, affected resource, and cryptographic signature.
REQ-103: In accordance with statutory banking regulations and financial anti-fraud requirements, all financial transactions, customer transaction histories, payment invoices, and monetary audit logs must be permanently retained for a minimum mandatory period of seven (7) years.
REQ-104: Under no circumstances may transaction history records, customer ledger references, or financial audit trails be deleted, purged, or truncated prior to the expiration of the statutory seven-year retention window.

[SECTION 2: AUTHENTICATION & ACCESS CONTROL]
REQ-105: Administrative functions and financial transfers exceeding $1,000 require two-factor authentication (2FA).
REQ-106: Session tokens must be securely stored and authenticated for all sensitive transactions.
REQ-107: Security audit logs must be reviewed by the compliance officer on an ongoing periodic basis.

[SECTION 3: SYSTEM NOTIFICATIONS]
REQ-108: Users must be immediately notified of any password reset or high-value monetary transaction via SMS or push alert within 15 seconds.
